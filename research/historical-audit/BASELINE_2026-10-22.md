**Issue:** [#75](https://github.com/BenWassa/Hermes/issues/75) · **Sample:** 30 latest recoverable publication editions, 2026-09-22 through 2026-10-22 · **Authority:** commit-pinned `docs/index.html` + its embedded `edition` JSON, with limited independent source checks.

The [full commit/date/blob index](HISTORY_INDEX.md) is the evidence locator. `research/historical-audit/` is owned by #75; the prospective Sentinel ledger and stable HQA IDs are owned by [#76](https://github.com/BenWassa/Hermes/issues/76).

### Quantitative denominator

- **30** distinct edition dates and pinned commit/HTML blob pairs. **626** ordinary section-story records in all: 29 editions × 21 and one × 17. The recurring empty `sports` section is a compatibility shell; the separate embedded `sports` payload is not empty by implication.
- **29/30** have a Toronto section (four slots per present section), **1/30** does not. The `following` feed is separate, typically six items; it is not included in 626.
- **23/30** persisted HTML build timestamps are **after 06:00**, **15/30** after 07:00, **6/30** after 08:00, **3/30** after noon in Toronto. Only **7/30** have a build timestamp at or before 06:00. These measure the saved build artifact, **not** independently verified GitHub Pages serve time. Build and commit timestamps differ by only a second in two cases.
- The **31-date** inclusive span contains **no** dated publication commit for **2026-10-20** in the queried main history. This is a source-history gap, not a proven 24-hour website outage.

## Findings with bounded confidence

### HA-01 — Retrospective date gap: October 20 (investigate; impact unknown)

## Confidence rules and what remains unmeasured

**Verified defect:** committed structure/label violates the stated page structure or shows a demonstrably inconsistent news/opinion or geographic label. **Verified observation, impact unknown:** timestamp, missing commit or repeated URL. **Candidate defect:** ranking choice, freshness or source/summary mismatch requiring comparison. **Unknown:** counterfactual omitted articles, unobserved API pool, model input/output trace, scheduled GitHub Action outcome without logs, Pages cache and real end-user delivery.

A later source corroborating an event **does not** prove it was discoverable at the edition's build time. To establish a material omission, require (1) an externally verified high-impact event, (2) an item published/indexable *before* that edition's build, (3) relevance to Hermes editorial policy, (4) proof of absence from that edition, and (5) a separate label for **available on the web** versus **available in Hermes' actual candidate pool**. Do not use post-publication corrections as if they were then known.

Recommended next calibration: re-audit **five stratified dates** (early/late builds, missing Toronto, local/nonlocal crossover, URL repeats) with explicit article-level source timestamps; log each claim as `verified / contradicted / unresolved`. Pair with [#74](https://github.com/BenWassa/Hermes/issues/74) prospective provenance to test future omissions and factual accuracy against actual candidate sets. Avoid claiming historical Gemini prompts, private reasoning or unseen ranking alternatives can be recovered.
